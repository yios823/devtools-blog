Pillar 1: Engineering Incident Management & Postmortems

The pillar in one paragraph

Incidents are inevitable. Postmortems are the part you control. This pillar covers how to write postmortems people actually read, how to set up incident response so the on-call rotation doesn’t collapse, and how the small habits (runbooks, SLOs, blameless language) compound into a team that breaks less often over time.

Most pieces here come out of real incidents. None of the examples are fabricated, none of the stats invented, none of the templates are theoretical: they were written down right after a thing broke and tuned by the next two or three breaks.

Note: I make some of the tools linked in this article. They’re optional; the guide stands on its own.
